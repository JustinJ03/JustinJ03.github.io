# Icons

Brand icon paths are supplied by the pinned `simple-icons` package (https://simpleicons.org / https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). They are rendered as inline SVG at build time; no icon CDN or third-party request is used by visitors.

Simple Icons is distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal. A copy is retained in [docs/simple-icons-license.md](docs/simple-icons-license.md). Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners and do not imply endorsement.

The city, project illustrations, game visuals, favicon, and general code-symbol fallback are original SVG/CSS artwork for this site. General symbols and parent-framework marks are visual aids, not claims that a product has a particular official logo.
